GRUG HAND

Four compiled TrueType faces:

- GrugHand-Light.ttf
- GrugHand-Regular.ttf
- GrugHand-Book.ttf
- GrugHand-Medium.ttf

Made by Jip van der Velde and Michel Elings at Ocho, creators of grug.
Downloaded from https://handdrawn.software/create/

The editable centerline drawing project is available separately from the font
forge. The TTF files contain compiled outlines and OpenType substitutions.
